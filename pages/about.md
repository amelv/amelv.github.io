<p>I'm a web developer and musician who enjoys turning complicated requirements into clear, dependable tools. I work with small businesses and nonprofits across the US. I run a woman- and LGBTQ-owned business.</p>

<p>You'll always work directly with me. The same person who builds your site sticks around to keep it running. I'll help you identify what you need, set everything up clearly, and be there when you have questions. You'll always know what's happening, what's next, and how to manage your site.</p>

<h2>How I Work</h2>

<p>I work with a small number of clients at a time, usually on an ongoing retainer basis. That means you get a consistent partner who understands your systems from launch through ongoing support.</p>

<p>Every project includes <strong>clear documentation</strong> so you or anyone else can understand how things work. If we stop working together, you'll have everything you need for a smooth handoff, including clear and portable documentation for your team or any future partner.</p>
