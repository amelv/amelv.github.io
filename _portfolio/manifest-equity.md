Redesigned the entire WordPress site for Manifest Equity and built the custom recommendation engine that powers their lead pipeline.

- Full site redesign with a custom Timber/Tailwind WordPress theme, dual-track hero sections for individual and business clients, and refreshed visual identity across all pages
- Developed a deterministic form-to-plan recommendation algorithm that qualifies leads and routes them to the right insurance products based on budget, needs, and business type
- Connected Gravity Forms submissions to Google Sheets and GoHighLevel CRM via webhooks for seamless lead tracking
